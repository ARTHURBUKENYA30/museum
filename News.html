Curator’s Talk: Ink & Independence

The museum invites the public to a curator-led talk on Ink & Independence, the current exhibition exploring how gazettes, newspapers and public notices recorded Uganda’s transition to independence and the early years of the Republic.

Research appointments open for Q4

The curatorial team is accepting research access requests for October–December 2026. Scholars, postgraduate students and independent researchers may request supervised consultation of designated gazettes, newspapers, photographic materials and other holdings approved for research use.

5 August 2026 · Collections

New oral history recordings accessioned

Three new interviews with former UPPC press operators have been accessioned into the Oral Histories & Trade Knowledge collection. The recordings document press-room routines, training pathways and the transition from letterpress practices toward newer production methods.
