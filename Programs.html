PROGRAMS

The museum offers structured learning for schools, universities and the public. Programmes connect collections with curriculum and curiosity — from a first encounter with letterpress to seminars that use original documents.

You can understand our education offer fully online before booking. The sections below describe who each programme is for, what happens during a session, and how to request a date.

School Workshops

Hands-on and discussion sessions for primary and secondary groups. Themes include how print worked, why gazettes and newspapers mattered, and craft skills behind public communication.

Typical length: 60–90 minutes · Group size: by arrangement

University Seminars

Support for courses in history, media studies, design and archival practice. Sessions can include gallery teaching and supervised handling of selected materials by appointment.

Booked with academic partners · Research rules apply

Public Talks

Curator talks, guest lectures and demonstration days open to the general public. Dates are listed under News and shared with mailing-list subscribers.

Usually free · Registration may be required

What a school visit includes

A standard school workshop combines a short introduction to the museum, time in the exhibition galleries, and a facilitated discussion or simple craft demonstration where space and staffing allow. Teachers receive a brief outline in advance so the visit can link to classroom work.

Please state age range, group size, preferred dates and any curriculum goals when you email. We need at least two weeks’ notice for most school bookings.

Professional & lifelong learning

Designers, heritage practitioners and independent learners may join public talks or request a focused introduction to type, machinery or official publications. Specialised sessions are subject to staff availability and collections care rules.

For research-level use of documents and oral histories, see rather than general programmes.

How to book

Email museum@uppc.co.ug with your institution, preferred dates, group size and learning aims. We will confirm what is possible and any preparation required on the day.
