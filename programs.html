Learning through print

Programs

Educational and public programs bring the history of printing and publishing to life through objects, stories and practical learning.

School learning visits

Introduce learners to the history of printing, the printed word and the role of public information.

Printing heritage talks

Talks and guided discussions explore historic processes, machines and the people who operated them.

Research and documentation

Support research into printing, publishing, institutional history and the preservation of archival material.
