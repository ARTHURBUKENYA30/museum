About

A national archive of print

The national repository for the material culture of printing and publishing in Uganda — collecting, conserving and interpreting tools, documents and stories since 1902.

We safeguard the instruments, publications and lived experience of printing in Uganda — from colonial gazettes to contemporary production.

“The press is the memory of a people. What is printed endures; what is preserved instructs.”
— Founding statement, 1902
