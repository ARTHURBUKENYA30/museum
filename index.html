Discover our heritage

Every printed page tells a story.

The UPPC Print Heritage Museum preserves the machines, materials and memories behind Uganda's printing and publishing journey. From early government printing to the newspapers, books and public records that shaped everyday life, our collections connect generations through the history of print.

Explore the craft of printing, discover historic publications and learn how printed information has helped document the country's changing story since 1902.
