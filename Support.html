SUPPORT THE MUSEUM

The museum relies on public engagement, partnerships and carefully managed contributions to conserve collections, develop exhibitions and run education programmes. All support is received under the framework of the Uganda Printing and Publishing Corporation.

Before you give time or resources, use this page to understand the main ways to help, what each pathway involves, and how to start a conversation with the museum team.

Donate

Financial contributions support conservation treatments, exhibition development, education programmes and basic collections care.

Gifts are acknowledged according to UPPC policy. Specific project funds can be discussed for conservation or education priorities.

Enquire about giving →

Partner

Cultural institutions, publishers, universities and civic organisations can collaborate on research, loans, co-curated displays and public programmes.

Partnerships are agreed in writing and respect collections care, credit and access rules.

Discuss partnership →

Volunteer

Skilled volunteers may assist with cataloguing support, guided visits and education activities, subject to training and supervision.

Roles depend on current projects and availability. A short induction is required before gallery or collections work.

Express interest →

Where support makes a difference

  • Conservation — stabilising fragile paper, documenting machinery and improving storage.
  • Exhibitions — research, design and interpretation for temporary and long-term displays.
  • Education — school workshops, public talks and materials for teachers.
  • Access — improving visitor information, research facilities and digital outreach such as this website and virtual tour.

In-kind gifts of objects

Offers of machinery, type, publications or photographs are welcomed in principle but must be assessed against the museum’s collecting policy, condition, space and long-term care capacity. Please do not send objects unsolicited. Write first with photographs and a short description.

Email museum@uppc.co.ug with the subject line “Object Offer”.
