SUPPORT THE MUSEUM

The museum relies on public engagement, partnerships and carefully managed contributions to conserve collections, develop exhibitions and run education programmes. All support is received under the framework of the Uganda Printing and Publishing Corporation.

Donate

Financial contributions support conservation treatments, exhibition development, education programmes and basic collections care.

Partner

Cultural institutions, publishers, universities and civic organisations can collaborate on research, loans, co-curated displays and public programmes.

Volunteer

Skilled volunteers may assist with cataloguing support, guided visits and education activities, subject to training and supervision.
